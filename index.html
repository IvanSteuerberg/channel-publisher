Channel Publisher

Channel Publisher is a private, personal tool used by its owner, Ivan Steuerberg, to upload and schedule videos on the YouTube channels he owns, and to read those channels' own statistics.

It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's account or data. It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API through Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0), only with the owner's own Google account.
